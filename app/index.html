THE IDEA BEHIND MAJIK MAKER

A symbol for your intention.

Choose a picture of something you want to welcome into your life. Add a few words, choose a geometry, and create your personal sigil.

Your image’s pixels and colors shape the pattern. Your words determine its unique arrangement. Orbital curves, wave interference, and connected forms provide the visual inspiration.

Inspired by science. A creative practice.

Quantum physics does not establish that thoughts or symbols manifest objects or change external events. This is art for reflection, visualization, and keeping your goals in focus.

Images are processed in your browser and are not uploaded or saved. Download a symbol you want to keep.

 TAKE YOUR INTENTIONS WITH YOU

Make room for a little magic.

One app, wherever you are.

Majik Maker opens in its own window on your phone or desktop. Once the app has loaded and offline setup is complete, you can create symbols without an internet connection.
